Cookie Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Yes — you need cookie consent.

Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 treats cookies and similar tracking technologies as personal data processing when they can identify a device or person, so non-essential cookies (analytics, advertising, embedded video/maps that track) need clear, informed, opt-in consent before they run — not just a notice. That's why this site's cookie banner blocks non-essential scripts until you click "Accept."

What are cookies?

Small text files stored on your device that let a website remember information between visits or requests.

Categories we use

Strictly necessary (always on)

Required for the site to function — keeping you logged in, remembering your cookie choice, and basic security. These don't require consent under the DPA because the site can't work without them, but we still tell you about them here.

Analytics (requires your consent)

Help us understand which pages and searches are used, so we can improve the site. These only load after you click "Accept all" on the cookie banner. If you click "Reject non-essential," these stay off.

Third-party embeds (requires your consent where they track you)

Some listings may embed third-party content — a 360° virtual-tour viewer, a map, or a chat widget. Some of these providers set their own cookies. We only load third-party embeds that set tracking cookies after you've consented; embeds that don't track you (e.g. a static map image) load normally. Before adding a new embed, check the provider's own cookie/privacy policy and update this list.

How to control cookies

  • Use the cookie banner shown on your first visit to accept or reject non-essential cookies.
  • Change your mind any time by clearing your browser's site data for this domain, which will show the banner again.
  • Most browsers also let you block cookies entirely in settings, though this may break some site features.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page if the cookies or embeds we use change.
