Refund Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Scope check needed before launch.

This policy covers fees paid to KejaSearch for platform features (e.g. a landlord's featured-listing upgrade). It deliberately does not cover rent or deposits, because KejaSearch does not collect those — confirm this matches your actual payment flow before publishing, and align wording with Kenya's Consumer Protection Act, 2012 if you sell any paid plan to consumers.

1. What this covers

This policy applies only to payments made directly to KejaSearch for platform services — for example, a landlord paying to feature a listing or unlock extra photos/tour slots.

2. What this does not cover

KejaSearch does not process rent, deposits, or any payment between a tenant and a landlord. If you paid a landlord directly, that payment is governed by your tenancy agreement with them, not by KejaSearch — see our Terms & Conditions. Report a landlord who has misused a deposit via the contact page; we can suspend their account, but we cannot reverse the payment for you.

3. Eligibility for a refund

  • A paid feature that was not delivered due to a technical fault on our side: full refund.
  • A paid feature cancelled within 48 hours of purchase and before it has been used (e.g. before a featured listing goes live): full refund.
  • A paid feature already delivered as described (e.g. your listing was featured for the period you paid for): not eligible for a refund.

4. How to request a refund

Email billing@nyumba360.example or use the contact form with your account email and payment reference. We aim to respond within 5 working days and, if approved, process refunds to the original payment method within 10 working days.

5. Your consumer rights

Nothing in this policy limits any right you have under Kenyan consumer protection law that cannot be excluded by agreement.
