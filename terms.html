This draft is written to be plain-language and to flag the main risk areas for a listings marketplace operating in Kenya, but it is not legal advice.

1. What KejaSearch is — and isn't

KejaSearch is a listings platform: we let landlords advertise houses and offices, and let tenants search, view 360° walkthroughs, contact landlords and leave reviews. We are not a letting agent, and we are not a party to any tenancy agreement between a landlord and a tenant. We do not inspect every property in person, and we do not guarantee the accuracy of any listing.

2. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old and able to form a binding contract under Kenyan law to create an account.

3. Landlord responsibilities

  • You confirm you own the property or have written authority (e.g. a management agreement) to list it.
  • Listing information (price, photos, 360° tour, description) must be accurate and kept up to date.
  • You are responsible for complying with Kenyan tenancy, health and safety, and tax law for any property you list.
  • A "Verified" badge means we checked the documents you supplied at a point in time — it is not a guarantee and can be revoked if we find false information.

4. Tenant responsibilities

  • Verify a property in person (or via video call) before paying any deposit or rent.
  • Reviews must reflect a genuine tenancy — do not post reviews for properties you have not lived in.
  • Treat landlords and other users respectfully; harassment or discriminatory messages may lead to account suspension.

5. Fees and payments

KejaSearch does not process rent, deposits, or payments between tenants and landlords. Any fees for using premium features of the platform itself are shown before you pay, and are covered by our Refund Policy.

6. Reviews and content moderation

We remove reviews that are fake, incentivized, defamatory, or posted by someone who cannot show a genuine tenancy. We remove listings that contain unsupported claims (e.g. false square footage, fabricated amenities) once reported and verified. Report a listing or review via the contact page.

7. Intellectual property

You retain ownership of photos, 360° tours and text you upload, but grant KejaSearch a licence to display them on the platform. You confirm you have the right to upload any image, floor plan or media you post — do not upload images you don't own or don't have permission to use. See "Copyright" in our compliance notes for landlords.

8. Limitation of liability

To the extent permitted by Kenyan law, KejaSearch is not liable for disputes, losses, or damages arising from a tenancy agreement, a landlord's conduct, or the accuracy of a listing. Our total liability for any claim relating to the platform itself is limited to the fees (if any) you paid us in the preceding 3 months.

9. Suspension and termination

We may suspend or remove an account or listing that breaches these terms, misrepresents a property, or is reported and confirmed as fraudulent.

10. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Kenya, and any dispute is subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the Kenyan courts.
