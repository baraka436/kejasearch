Verification

Verified landlords appear higher in search and carry the ✓ Verified badge. Verification requires:

  • A valid national ID or passport
  • Proof of ownership or a signed management agreement for each listed property
  • A phone number confirmed by SMS/call

Documents are used only to confirm your identity and right to list the property, and are handled under our Privacy Policy.
