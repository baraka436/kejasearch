Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

This is a starting template, not finished legal advice.

Have a Kenyan advocate review this before launch, and register with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) if the Act requires it for your organisation. This page is written to be plain-language and DPA-aware, but it is not a substitute for professional legal review.

1. Who we are

KejaSearch Ltd ("KejaSearch", "we", "us") operates this website to connect tenants looking for houses and offices with landlords in Nairobi, Kenya. We are the data controller for personal data collected through this site, as defined under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019 ("the DPA").

2. What we collect

We only collect data we need to run the service:

  • Account data: name, email, phone number, password (stored hashed), and account type (tenant or landlord).
  • Landlord verification data: national ID/passport number, and proof of ownership or a management agreement, used only to confirm identity and right to list a property.
  • Listing data: property address, description, price, photos and 360° walkthrough media supplied by landlords.
  • Reviews: the review text, star rating and reviewer name you choose to display.
  • Messages: anything you submit through contact or enquiry forms.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and pages visited, collected through cookies — see our Cookie Policy for what requires your consent.

We do not knowingly collect more than this, and we do not collect sensitive personal data (health, biometric, etc.) unless a future feature explicitly asks for it with separate consent.

3. Why we use it

  • To create and secure your account, and verify landlord identity.
  • To show your listing to tenants, or connect you with a landlord.
  • To moderate reviews and remove fake, incentivized or unverified ones.
  • To respond to enquiries, complaints and data requests.
  • With your separate consent: to send you product updates and to run analytics cookies.

4. Legal basis for processing

We process your data based on: your consent (e.g. marketing emails, analytics cookies), the necessity of processing to perform our contract with you (e.g. hosting your listing, running your account), and our legitimate interest in keeping the platform safe (e.g. fraud and fake-review checks).

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited data with:

  • Other users, where the site is designed to show it — e.g. a landlord's name and phone number is shown on their own listings so tenants can contact them directly.
  • Service providers who host the site, send emails, or provide the 360° walkthrough player, bound by contract to protect your data.
  • Authorities, where required by Kenyan law.

Any transfer of personal data outside Kenya is only made with appropriate safeguards or your consent, as required by section 48 of the DPA.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account data for as long as your account is active, and listing/review data for as long as it is relevant to other users, after which it is deleted or anonymised unless we're required to keep it longer by law (e.g. financial records).

7. Your rights

Under the DPA, you have the right to: be informed of how your data is used, access the data we hold on you, correct inaccurate data, object to processing, request erasure, and request portability. To exercise any of these, email dpo@nyumba360.example or use the contact form. We aim to respond within the timeframes set by the DPA.

8. Cookies

See our separate Cookie Policy for details on what we use and how to control it.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures (encryption in transit, access controls, hashed passwords) to protect your data, but no system is 100% secure. If we experience a data breach likely to harm you, we will notify the ODPC and affected users as required by the DPA.

10. Children

This service is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll post any changes here with an updated date, and notify registered users of material changes.

12. Contact & complaints

Email dpo@nyumba360.example. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner, Kenya.
